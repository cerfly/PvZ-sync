## Before sending — checklist

- [ ] Replace `YOUR_EMAIL…` with the real email (use a fresh / unused EA-registerable address).
- [ ] Attach a screenshot of your phone's profile + the exact coin/gem numbers above.
- [ ] Send through **help.ea.com → PvZ2 → Contact us** (choose "Account / progress issue").
- [ ] Keep the case number; reply to the same thread if they ask follow-ups.

## Likely replies & how to respond

1. **"Do the linking from inside the game (cloud icon)."**
   Respond: the icon is not shown on my already-online profile — that is exactly the
   problem; please either surface the linking UI server-side or do the link on your end
   using the GIN above.
2. **"We can't change anonymous accounts."**
   Ask: can you at least reset my tablet-side identity so the linking icon reappears
   on my *phone*, where I can complete a fresh in-game registration? (Geo-routing is
   available on the phone, so registration there is reliable.)
3. **No reply in 3–5 business days.**
   Follow up on the same ticket, quoting the GIN and case number, and attach proof
   (profile screenshot + coin/gem stats).
